# Umbrella Quest graphics manifest

These seven PNG atlases are the recovered ImageGen 2 production sources from the interrupted “Finish Umbrella Adventure Graphics” task. Their pixels are preserved; runtime presentation uses `AtlasTexture` crops defined in `atlas_regions.gd`.

- `art/characters/pc3/pc3_walk.png` — side, front, and rear four-frame walk cycles.
- `art/characters/pc3/pc3_motion.png` — investigation and conversation gestures.
- `art/characters/pc3/pc3_combat.png` — bounded combat actions, reactions, and portrait.
- `art/demo/cast/alex_atlas.png` — three room poses and three matching portraits.
- `art/demo/cast/mina_atlas.png` — three room poses and three matching portraits.
- `art/demo/cast/intruder_atlas.png` — loading-bay opponent actions, reactions, and portrait.
- `art/demo/objects/evidence_atlas.png` — umbrella, ticket/tag, records, timing, and physical traces.

The accepted eight environment paintings and `pc3_reference_atlas_hd.webp` remain unchanged. Player animation changes only the child visual: the 42×82 gameplay footprint, foot coordinate, approach points, transitions, and saved position remain authoritative.

The raw recovered chat downloads, including two rejected variants, remain in `art/recovered/finish_umbrella_graphics/` for provenance and repair work.
