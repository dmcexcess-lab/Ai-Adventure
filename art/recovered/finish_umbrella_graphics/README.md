# Recovered Finish Umbrella Adventure Graphics assets

These nine PNG assets were recovered directly from the expanded work/output sections of the ChatGPT conversation **Finish Umbrella Adventure Graphics** on 2026-10-02.

They were generated during an unfinished graphics pass but were not committed to the repository or exposed through the task-history API.

## Assets

- `pc3_walk_interact_sheet_original_bg.png` — original 24-frame PC/#3 sheet; retained for provenance even though its dark background makes it unsuitable for direct runtime use.
- `pc3_walk_interact_sheet_transparent.png` — corrected transparent PC/#3 walk and interaction sheet.
- `pc3_walk_cycles_transparent.png` — transparent side/front/rear walk-cycle sheet.
- `pc3_combat_pose_atlas.png` — PC/#3 combat, reaction, and portrait poses.
- `alex_pose_portrait_atlas.png` — Alex full-body poses and matching dialogue portraits.
- `mina_pose_portrait_atlas.png` — Mina full-body poses and matching dialogue portraits.
- `loading_bay_opponent_atlas_a.png` — loading-bay opponent combat atlas, first recovered version.
- `loading_bay_opponent_atlas_b.png` — loading-bay opponent combat atlas, alternate corrected version.
- `evidence_object_atlas.png` — umbrella, tags, log/handbook, clock, wet sleeve, tape, hook, scuff, and trace graphics.

## Integration status

These are original recovered source atlases. They have been preserved without cropping or destructive processing. They are not yet connected to the Godot scenes; integration should crop individual frames, confirm transparent edges and safe bounds, then replace the live prototype SVG resources while retaining the existing gameplay coordinates and logic.
