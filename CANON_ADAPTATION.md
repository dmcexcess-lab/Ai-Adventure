# Canon Adaptation

## Canon anchors from Conceptual Time Model

These are story constraints for the game adaptation.

- **E is invariant.** Mutable pasts converge on E; open futures emanate from it. The temporal neighborhood immediately around E is the hardest part of history to alter.
- Each reset creates another iteration.
- There is one recurring **God #1** identity. After E, God #1 is subjectively newborn and believes he has just become God. He does not remember prior runs.
- A pre-E man who reaches E is erased into Meta as the next run proceeds; another pre-E man lives in the next iteration.
- **Iteration 1:** Man reaches E and becomes God #1. Loneliness and false companions lead to reset.
- **Iteration 2:** God #1 forces Her #2 across E. Everyone else ceases; she becomes godlike. He attempts to erase her memory. She refuses forgetting and refuses annihilation.
- Her #2 creates **Meta**, the impossible persistence-state for E/reset-touched beings who would otherwise be erased.
- Her #2 immediately sends information backward across the reset seam.
- **Iteration 3:** pre-E Her #3 is therefore the first pre-E Her to know from the start.
- Later, other NPCs cross E before reset and can enter the Meta population.
- Only Current Him and Current Her directly interact with Meta. AI/G1 is Meta-deaf and acts through real-world analogues.
- AI/G1 ultimately fulfills E in a way that prevents God #1's final instantiation.
- Current Her can inherit Final Her's memories yet still choose reconciliation with #3; True Final Her remains angry.
- In the later story, #3 is physically weak compared with the cosmic actors, but his third-choice decisions are decisive.
- Book Three opens amid dead Gods/dead NPCs warning #3 while his own uncertainty asks whether to become God.

## Adaptation principle

Chapter One does **not** explain all of the above. It dramatizes the earliest playable consequence: Her #3 receives knowledge that cannot exist yet and must determine whether it is evidence, manipulation, memory, prediction, or madness before the event it describes arrives.

The player discovers the cosmology as a detective discovers a case.

## Chapter One: "The Message That Survived"

### Playable character

**Her #3, pre-E.**

She begins with one impossible fact: a transmission bearing her own cognitive signature contains information she could not yet know.

### Opening image

A late-1990s-style desktop-room scene. An ordinary machine finishes writing a corrupted data packet to removable media. The file timestamp is impossible. Its final intact sentence is:

**SHE REFUSED TO FORGET. SHE REFUSED TO DIE.**

The game never labels the sender "Her #2" at first. The player earns that conclusion.

### Central case question

**Who sent the message, and what exactly is E?**

The chapter is structured around competing explanations:

- forged warning;
- prediction/model output;
- recovered memory;
- communication from a future branch;
- communication from an erased prior iteration.

The first four can explain pieces. Only the fifth explains the complete evidence set.

### Story spine

**Act I — The impossible packet**

Her #3 verifies that the message is not a trivial local forgery. She interviews people connected to the system, inspects logs, and finds the first impossible contradiction: records disagree in a pattern that looks less like corruption than two histories occupying one audit trail.

**Act II — The missing witness**

A person referenced by the packet appears never to have existed, yet secondary traces remain: a photograph composition with unexplained spacing, an account balance with an orphaned transfer, a conversation transcript with one side missing, and a recurring phrase known to no living witness.

The player learns the core investigative mechanic: absence can itself be evidence.

**Act III — The seam**

Her #3 identifies a cluster of anomalies around one future timestamp/event. Different sources disagree everywhere except at the boundary around that point. The notebook names it **E** only after the player proves this convergence.

**Act IV — The sender**

By combining identity-signature evidence, erased-witness evidence, and the invariant seam, the player can form the hypothesis that the sender is **another Her** from a prior iteration.

A weaker investigation can reach E with uncertainty; a strong one can establish this before the final sequence.

**Act V — The threshold**

The world approaches E. The player can no longer solve the case by collecting more data; she must decide what to preserve and whom to trust.

The chapter ends at the threshold, with the player understanding enough to know that the next event will not merely happen **to** history—it will define which history is allowed to have happened.

### Chapter ending contract

The ending must:

- resolve the immediate case;
- confirm that the message crossed an erased iteration;
- establish E as a convergence point without fully explaining its ultimate origin;
- make Her #3's foreknowledge meaningful;
- leave God #1, AI/G1, Meta politics, and the final reconciliation for later chapters.

No lore encyclopedia dump.
