- Desktop Firefox target.

- Detective/evidence gameplay is primary.
- Inventory is secondary and never the main puzzle grammar.

4. Show contradictions converge around one future boundary.
5. Establish that E is more invariant than surrounding history.
6. Determine that the sender shares Her's identity signature.
7. Rule out a single continuous future.
8. Infer an erased prior iteration.
9. Reach E with evidence-dependent understanding.
